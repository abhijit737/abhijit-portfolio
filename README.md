## Interactive 3D Features

### Three.js WebGL Background

- More than 1,200 glowing particles in mint teal, azure blue, and cyber violet.
- Floating wireframe icosahedron and torus knot meshes.
- Smooth mouse parallax moves the 3D camera as you move the pointer.
- The **3D LIVE** button in the navigation bar switches the particle speed to 3× or 5×.

### Holographic Avatar

- The profile photo (`abhijit.jpg`) sits at the center of three rotating rings, with a horizontal holographic scanline.
- Six technology chips orbit the avatar with 3D depth: **Mobile Apps**, **C# / .NET 8**, **Azure Cloud**, **Microservices**, **React & TypeScript**, and **SQL Server**.
- The avatar tilts in response to pointer movement.

### Card and Button Interactions

- Portfolio cards tilt on hover using `perspective(1000px)`. Icons, titles, and badges lift off the card surface with `translateZ(30px)`.
- A radial spotlight follows the pointer across each card.
- A glowing emerald cursor follows the pointer with spring-like motion and scales on hover over links and cards.
- Raised buttons move downward when pressed.
